Chart Coaster

Every chart is a track. Pick a crash, a bubble or a squeeze and ride it from the front car. Rallies are the slow chain lift. Sell-offs are the drop. Physics is real: the steeper the collapse, the faster you go and the more airtime you get.

Ride your own chart

One point per line: date, value or just value. Dates like 2024-03-01 space the track by real time; plain values are spaced evenly. Paste a CSV column straight from a spreadsheet.

Drag to look around. Space pauses. Values are approximate closing prices at key dates, smoothly interpolated in between, so the shape is right but small wiggles are simplified. The CO₂ ride is modelled from the Mauna Loa trend and its yearly seasonal cycle.
