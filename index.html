Chart Coaster

Every chart is a track. Pick a crash, a bubble or a squeeze and ride it from the front car, through the city where it happened, with a soundtrack from the era. Rallies are the slow chain lift. Sell-offs are the drop. Billboards along the way tell you what was going on. Tap a ride to hear its music.

Ride your own chart

One point per line: date, value or just value. Dates like 2024-03-01 space the track by real time; plain values are spaced evenly. Paste a CSV column straight from a spreadsheet.

Drag to look around. Space pauses. The music turns darker when the chart is more than 20% below its high. All music is original, generated live in your browser. Values are approximate closing prices at key dates, smoothly interpolated in between, so the shape is right but small wiggles are simplified. The CO₂ ride is modelled from the Mauna Loa trend and its yearly seasonal cycle.
